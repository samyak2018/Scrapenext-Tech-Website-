About US

We help businesses digitize effectively

Be it building a website or transitioning to a digital solution, ScrapeNext Technologies helps businesses with a complete one-stop solution model tailored to their needs, budget, and goals.

Since 2018, ScrapeNext has been delivering digital solutions to clients across 4 continents. Starting with services like mobile app development, digital marketing, and customized printing, we later built a platform for 1NGO, enabling 1000+ NGOs to create their own websites.

Over time, we identified our core strength in Web Development and focused on creating impactful solutions. We have successfully built 100+ websites across industries including finance, travel & hospitality, food & beverages, healthcare, AI, education, and more.
